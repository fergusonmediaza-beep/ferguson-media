Finding the Funny in the Everyday: Themba Robin on Comedy & Positivity

South African comedian and content creator Themba Robin joins host Jack Devnarain to talk about his "childlike lens" philosophy and how finding the funny keeps communities connected.

About This Episode

Themba Robin is a South African comedian and content creator who has built a following by doing something simple but rare: looking at the frustrations and absurdities of everyday life and choosing to laugh. In this episode, he sits down with host Jack Devnarain to unpack that instinct — where it comes from, how it informs his creative work, and why he believes positivity is a form of resistance.

Themba talks about growing up, developing his comedic voice, and finding an audience that doesn't just laugh at his content but feels seen by it. The conversation explores how humour can be a powerful vehicle for connection in a country with so many divisions.

"Comedy isn't about avoiding the hard stuff. It's about finding the light inside it — and showing other people it's there too."

The Childlike Lens

At the core of Themba's philosophy is what he calls the "childlike lens" — approaching the world with curiosity rather than cynicism. He explains how this mindset shapes not just his content, but the way he engages with his community, collaborates with other creators, and approaches the business of being a South African creative in a digital first era.

Jack draws out the personal discipline behind the creative output: the daily choices Themba makes to stay grounded, stay positive, and keep creating even when the algorithm or the audience doesn't respond the way he hoped.

Why Comedy Matters in South Africa

This conversation is a reminder that good news doesn't always arrive as a headline. Sometimes it arrives as a laugh — shared between strangers, passed between followers, felt in a moment of recognition. Themba Robin is building that space, one video at a time.
